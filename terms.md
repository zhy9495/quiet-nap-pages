# Terms

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Quiet Nap is a lifestyle app for short rests, ambient sound, and gentle reminders.

Quiet Nap is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or sleep disorder management. If you have persistent insomnia, excessive sleepiness, sleep apnea, anxiety, pain, or other health concerns, consult a qualified medical professional.

Reminder behavior can be affected by iOS Focus, Do Not Disturb, Low Power Mode, notification permission, the silent switch, system volume, Bluetooth routing, and headphone connection state.
