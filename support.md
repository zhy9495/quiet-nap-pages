# Support

Quiet Nap uses local audio, vibration, and local notifications for nap reminders.

If reminders, sounds, or notifications behave unexpectedly, check:

- Notification permission
- Focus or Do Not Disturb settings
- Silent switch
- System volume
- Bluetooth devices
- Headphone connection state
- Low Power Mode

For important naps, run a short test first to confirm your device settings.

## Reminder Reliability

Quiet Nap uses background audio during a nap to keep reminders available, then plays a wake sound or starts vibration when the timer ends.

If Headphones Only is enabled and no headphones are connected, the app will not play sound through the speaker. If vibration is enabled, it will use vibration instead.

iOS Focus, Do Not Disturb, Low Power Mode, notification permission, the silent switch, Bluetooth routing, and system volume can all affect reminders.
